About BrickJ FMTB

Hi, I’m the creator behind FingerMTB. I started this project out of pure passion for building, designing, and riding tiny bikes and skis. Every single thing you see on this website is made by me — from the finger bikes and finger skis to the obstacles, rails, and accessories. All products are designed, tested, and improved by hand, and every model is created with attention to detail, functionality, and fun. I also built this entire website myself, from the design to the code, to have full control over how the brand looks and feels. FingerMTB is about creativity, learning by doing, and proving that small ideas can turn into something real. Mini bikes. Big tricks. Built by one person.
